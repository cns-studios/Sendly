2. End-to-End Encryption & Data Security

Our Service utilizes client-side End-to-End (E2E) encryption. Files are encrypted in your browser before they reach our servers. We store only the encrypted binary blobs and do not possess the decryption keys. The decryption key is generated on your device. For share links, it is contained solely within the URL fragment (the part after the #) of the link. For files sent directly to another Sendly user, it is encrypted on your device for that user's account key, so only the recipient's approved devices can unlock it.
