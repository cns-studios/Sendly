### What end-to-end encryption means for moderation

Files are encrypted in your browser and we never hold the keys. We therefore cannot view, scan, filter or search file contents, and we do not use automated tools to analyse them. As a result:

- we can act on a specific file only when a report or notice identifies it (by link, file ID, 12-digit code or the in-app report button)
- we generally cannot verify what a reported file contains, and we cannot find other copies of the same content
- where a notice concerns content we cannot pinpoint, for example a file sent between users, we act on the account or feature involved instead

Every report is forwarded to our moderators and, to the extent our technical limits allow, reviewed by a person. If a notice identifies a specific file and gives sufficient reason to believe it is illegal, we delete it. Deletion is permanent and applies to everyone the file was shared with.

A file is also deleted automatically, without prior human review, once several different signed-in users have reported it (currently three). Reports from anonymous users are reviewed but never trigger automatic deletion on their own. Where we become aware of information suggesting a criminal offence that threatens the life or safety of persons, we inform the competent authorities.
